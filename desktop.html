How to sync all your devices (iPhone, iPad, Desktop)
1. On your FIRST device only: paste a GitHub token → Connect. The app creates its own cloud storage — done.
2. On every other device: open Sync here → scan the QR code with the phone's camera (it opens the app already connected), or copy the pairing code and paste it into that device's Sync screen.
3. That's it — everything syncs automatically from then on (~8s after any change, plus every time an app opens).
